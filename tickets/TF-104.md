## Resolution

Implemented in `demo-app` per `taskflow-api-docs/api-conventions.md`
("Pagination"): `cursor`/`limit` parameters, `next_cursor` in the response,
limit capped at 200. Offset pagination was not used — it skips or repeats
rows under concurrent writes.
